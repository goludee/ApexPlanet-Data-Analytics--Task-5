# 3–5 Minute Portfolio Walkthrough Script

Hello everyone. This is my ApexPlanet Data Analytics Internship master portfolio.

This repository brings together my work across the internship into one place.

Task 1 focused on data preparation and exploratory analysis. I learned how to inspect, clean, and understand structured data before drawing conclusions.

Task 2 moved toward business analysis, where I focused on extracting useful patterns, metrics, and insights.

Task 3 was a deep-dive analysis and interactive dashboarding project. I defined key KPIs such as total sales, unique orders, unique customers, average order value, and units per order. I also performed customer segmentation using recency and monetary value and built an interactive dashboard.

Task 4 focused on data storytelling and statistical validation. I created a stakeholder-oriented presentation and applied a Welch independent two-sample t-test. The p-value was approximately 0.495, so the dataset did not provide statistically significant evidence of a difference in mean order sales between female and male customer records at the 5 percent level.

Task 5 brings all of this work together as a professional portfolio.

My biggest learning was understanding the complete analytics journey: from raw data and cleaning, through analysis and visualization, to statistical validation and business communication.

Thank you for watching my portfolio walkthrough.
