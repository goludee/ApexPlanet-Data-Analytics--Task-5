## Key Skills

## Task 3 Highlights
Defined core sales KPIs, performed customer segmentation using recency and monetary value, and built an interactive dashboard.

## Task 4 Highlights
Created a business data story and presentation and validated a business hypothesis with an independent Welch two-sample t-test. The test comparing female and male order sales produced p = 0.4950; the 95% CI for Male − Female mean difference was approximately ₹-9,231.58 to ₹19,079.85. At the 5% significance level, the dataset did not provide statistically significant evidence of a difference in mean order sales.

## Final Reflection
The internship strengthened my ability to move from raw data to business insight: prepare data, define KPIs, analyze patterns, visualize findings, validate evidence statistically, and communicate results clearly.
